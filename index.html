Switch Roll Privacy Policy

Switch Roll for Android · Effective 25 November 2026

In short

  • Switch Roll has no accounts, and your game progress stays on your device. We have no servers and cannot see it.
  • The game is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects device information to show those ads, as explained below.
  • If you are in a region where consent is required, you choose whether ads can be personalised, and you can change your choice later.
  • We use Google services to count how the game is played and to report crashes, so we can fix problems and improve the game.

This policy explains what information Switch Roll handles and what choices you have. "We" and "us" means Bilal Azmat, the developer of Switch Roll, publishing as Pebblefin.

1. Your game progress

Switch Roll saves your progress so you can carry on playing. This includes your best scores, coins, level, the ball skins you own, your missions, your Daily Run results, your login streak, your stats and your settings (music, sound effects, vibration and reduced motion). The Daily Run road is generated on your device from the date. All of this is stored inside the app on your device and is not sent to us.

You do not need to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other contact details in the game.

Android may include this saved game in your device's own backup if you have backups turned on. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account, and we cannot access it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Switch Roll shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Some are optional: you can choose to watch an ad for a reward, such as continuing a run after a crash, doubling the coins from a run, opening a free coin chest or swapping a mission. Others are a banner on the menu screens and occasional full-screen ads shown between runs. No ad is shown while you are playing a run.

To show ads, measure them and help prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other identifiers;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be worked out;
  • device and app details, such as the device model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads, including ads viewed and tapped, and crash and performance information.

Google, and where you allow it its advertising partners, may use this information to show ads that are personalised to you. Android's advertising features, such as Topics, may also be used to choose ads. Without consent where it is required, only non-personalised or limited ads are requested. These can still use some device information for purposes such as frequency limits, fraud prevention and measurement.

We do not receive information that identifies you personally from Google. We see only overall reports, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned.

You can read more in Google's Privacy Policy and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Your ad choices

  • Europe and the UK: if you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • US states with privacy laws: if you live in one of these states, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data under Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Your device: you can delete or reset your advertising ID, and manage ad topics and related features, in your Android settings. On many devices these are under Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Google: you can manage personalised ads at My Ad Center.

4. Purchases (Remove Ads)

You can buy Remove Ads inside the game. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google Play tells the game only whether you own Remove Ads, so it can turn off the banners and the ads between runs, including after you reinstall (Settings → Restore purchases).

5. Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your scores can be posted to leaderboards and your achievements unlocked. Google Play Games then handles your gamer profile, scores and achievements under Google's own policies. Leaderboards may show your Play Games name and score to other players. You can play without signing in.

6. Analytics and crash reports

To understand how the game is played and fix problems, Switch Roll may send anonymous usage events and crash reports to Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics). These include, for example, when a run starts and ends, the score and zone reached, which features are used, the device model and operating system, and details of any crash. They use an app instance identifier, not your name or contact details. Google processes this data under Firebase's privacy terms.

7. Phone permissions

Switch Roll uses internet access, network status and the advertising ID for the ads and services described above, billing for the optional purchase, and vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. The ads SDK also uses a few standard background permissions to work properly. The game does not ask for access to your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Switch Roll removes it. A device backup may keep a copy until that backup is replaced or deleted. Because we do not receive your progress, we cannot restore it for you. Information collected for ads, purchases, Play Games, analytics and crash reports is kept under Google's own policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe and the UK, or US state laws such as California's), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to object to or opt out of certain uses, including targeted advertising. We do not hold personal information about you on our own systems, except what you send us if you contact us. You can use the ad choices in section 3, and Google's tools for information it collects.

We do not sell your information for money, but the sharing described in section 2 may count as "selling" or "sharing" under some US state laws. Your Android settings and the choices above let you limit it.

10. Children

Switch Roll is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
